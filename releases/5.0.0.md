# PlexonCrates 5.0.0

Stable GitHub repository closure of the accepted PlexonCrates Phase 2 / Phase 3 premium crate line and final RC4 UX, plus one durability correction discovered during the final source audit.

## Opening and transaction safety

- Journal-first openings retain per-player locks, immutable opening plans, immediate state/revision revalidation, exact key accounting and explicit payment/grant recovery stages.
- Physical, local virtual-key and PlexonKeys wallet payment paths remain idempotent and auditable.
- Reward limits, pity, milestones and inventory capacity are revalidated immediately before consumption/delivery.
- Exact `ItemStack` custody remains byte-snapshot based with SHA-256 verification and bounded Claim Inbox recovery.
- Portable crates remain HMAC-signed, durable-issuance-backed, owner/revision checked, reservation-based and single-use.
- Rerolls remain journaled and keep the current candidate until a replacement and payment transition are accepted.

## Final source-audit durability fix

A successfully credited **virtual-key Claim Inbox** entry now moves immediately to `REVIEW` when claim finalization is uncertain or fails. Previously the player was warned but the durable row could remain `CLAIMING` until the next server restart, where startup recovery would eventually move it to review.

This change aligns virtual-key claim handling with exact-item claims: once an external/inventory side effect may have occurred, the claim fails closed into administrator review and is never automatically retried.

## Premium 5.0 product line

- Percentage-first rewards use exact integer basis points and eligible-pool normalization.
- Selective rewards remain deliberate player choices and never fabricate random percentages.
- Crate Hall, reward preview, linked-crate preview and selective confirmation use the accepted Phase 3 player-facing probability/fallback language.
- Durable drafts, leases, stale-action guards, publication validation and immutable runtime revisions remain authoritative for administration.
- Statistics, limits, pity, milestones, rerolls, virtual-key ledgers, Claim Inbox entries and portable issuance remain SQLite-owned.
- The four historical `MANUAL_REVIEW` opening-journal rows remain untouched.
